Store Owner Configuration Guide

How to replace placeholders and publish your website

1. WhatsApp & Phone Numbers to Replace

In both index.html and script.js (inside SHOP_CONFIG at line 10), replace the sample number 94770000000 with your registered Sri Lankan phone number (in international format without '+' or spaces, e.g. 94771234567).

const SHOP_CONFIG = {
  businessName: 'LinkPoint',
  phoneDisplay: '+94 77 123 4567',
  phoneCall: '+94771234567',
  whatsappNumber: '94771234567',
  addressText: 'Your Real Street Address, Town, Sri Lanka'
};
2. Physical Shop Address & Google Maps

Update the shop address placeholder in index.html under the #contact section. To show your live map, go to Google Maps, search your shop, click Share → Embed a map, and paste the <iframe> code into .map-embed-wrapper.

3. Demonstration Products & Pricing

All smartphone and accessory models are configured in script.js in the DEMO_PRODUCTS array. Update model names, specifications, and prices (in LKR) with your verified shop stock.

4. Printing Rates

Printing and finishing rates are stored in PRINT_RATES and FINISHING_RATES in script.js. Adjust the per-page rate to match your current paper and toner costs.
